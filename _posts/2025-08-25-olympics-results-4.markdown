# Olympic History Dashboard – Tableau Project

This project explores **120 years of Olympic Games history (1896–2012)** using the Kaggle dataset [120 Years of Olympic History: Athletes and Results](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/heesoo37/120-years-of-olympic-history-athletes-and-results).  
It was built as an interactive **Tableau dashboard** to practice data visualization and storytelling with real-world data.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

- **Objective:**  
  Analyze global Olympic medal trends, top-performing athletes and countries, and focus on Hungary’s historical results.
  
- **Key Questions:**
  - Which countries dominated Olympic history?
  - Who are the top medal-winning athletes?
  - How did medal counts evolve over time?
  - What is Hungary’s performance compared to global trends?

---

## 🗂 Dataset

- Source: [Kaggle – 120 Years of Olympic History](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/heesoo37/120-years-of-olympic-history-athletes-and-results)
- Data covered: 1896 to 2012 Summer and Winter Olympics
- Includes: Athletes, country (NOC), gender, year, sport, event, medal type

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features & Insights

The Tableau dashboard includes:
- **World Map:** Medals by country (filterable by year, region, and income group)
- **Top 10 Countries Medal Trends:** Running total of medals since 1896
- **Top Medal-Winning Athletes:** Highlighting legends like Michael Phelps & Larisa Latynina
- **Hungarian Performance:** Line chart comparing Hungarian medals vs global totals
- **Sports Heatmap:** Medal count by year and sport (e.g., fencing, aquatics, gymnastics)
- **Gender Trends:** Breakdown of medals by men vs women over time

**Key Insights:**
- USA leads historically with the highest medal count.
- Strong historical performances by USSR, GBR, FRA, GER, and CHN.
- Hungary has an outstanding legacy in fencing and aquatics.
- Women’s participation and medal counts grew significantly after mid-20th century.
